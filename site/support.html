Common questions

Where is my data?

On your phone only. Deleting the app, or Settings, Reset everything, erases it.

How do I change what an HSP costs?

Settings, Money and units. The default is $18, which is a fair Melbourne price for a large with the lot.

Can I turn the roasts down?

Yes. Settings, Roast level: Mild, Medium, or Unhinged (adults only).

How do I export a month?

Settings, Export. You get a spreadsheet, a PDF report, or a shareable HSP receipt.

How do I log Apple Pay buys automatically?

Settings, Apple Pay shortcut walks you through a two minute Shortcuts automation.
