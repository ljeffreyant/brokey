## First-hour routine (10 minutes, each day)

1. Post the reel, add the pinned comment, pin it.
2. Reply to every comment for the first hour. Replies count as more comments and bring people back.
3. Share the reel to your Story with a "Part 2 tomorrow" text sticker, so followers who missed it catch up.
4. On day 2 and 3, put the previous part in your Story too, so a new viewer can start from Part 1.
5. After Part 3, pin all three to the top of your profile in order (Part 1 first).

## Check before posting

- Watch each reel on your own phone with sound on. I could not listen to the audio, so tell me if anything is too loud, too quiet, or the narrator feels off.
- The burned-in captions sit around y 1290 to 1520 on the 1920 tall frame. That is above Instagram's usual username and caption block but inside Meta's conservative "bottom 35%" zone. If the right-hand buttons or your caption cover them on your phone, tell me and I will move them up.
