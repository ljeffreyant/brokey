## Daily routine (5 minutes)

1. Post at about 7pm, paste the caption, add the alt text, post, then paste the first comment and pin it.
2. Reply to every comment for the first hour.
3. Share the post to your Story with a sticker (poll: "Mild, medium or unhinged?" works well on day 3).
4. Reuse each image on TikTok as a photo post, or as a 3 second clip with a trending sound.

## Check before posting

- The bio link: change `Link in bio` lines only once the link works. Until then, leave "Follow so you do not miss it" as the call to action.
- If you want a different date on the schedule, nothing on the images depends on it.
