# Brokey "coming soon" teasers: post kit (copy and paste)

Six static posts, 1080x1440 (3:4, the exact shape of the profile grid, so nothing is cropped). One a day, about 7pm Melbourne time. Post them in order 1 to 6. Every number, roast and quip on the images comes from the app itself.

The idea: the series asks "how many snack packs is that?" and answers it with the app's own numbers. The numbers climb (1, 2.1, 9.9, 14), then the weekly fries run low (3 left), then the reveal. Odd posts are yellow, even posts are ink, and the lines run edge to edge, so the grid reads as one checkerboard ledger. `SYSTEM.png` shows the logic on one page.

Rules baked in: five hashtags max (Instagram's limit), hook in the first line, no link in captions (it is not clickable), and "Link in bio" only once the bio link is live. Alt text is included because it helps people using screen readers and costs nothing (Instagram: Advanced settings > Write alt text).

## Daily routine (5 minutes)

1. Post at about 7pm, paste the caption, add the alt text, post, then paste the first comment and pin it.
2. Reply to every comment for the first hour.
3. Share the post to your Story with a sticker (a poll like "Mild, medium or unhinged?" works well on day 2 or 3).
4. Reuse each image on TikTok as a photo post, or as a 3 second clip with a trending sound.

## Check before posting

- If your profile already has other posts, the checkerboard starts at post 1, so it will still look intentional, just with older posts below it.
- The bio link: only say "Link in bio" once the link works. Until then, leave "Follow so you do not miss it" as the call to action.
- If you want different dates, nothing on the images depends on them.
