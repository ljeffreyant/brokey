# Brokey "coming soon" teasers: post kit (copy and paste)

Six illustrated posts, 1080x1440 (3:4, the exact shape of the profile grid, so nothing is cropped). One a day, about 7pm Melbourne time. Post them in order 1 to 6.

The idea: the six posts are one painting of the Brokey kebab shop at night. The bottom row is the street (shopfront, price board window, tram shelter ad). The top row is inside (the fridge, the counter with 3 fries left, the phone with the app). Instagram puts your newest post top left, so as you post 1 to 6 the street appears first and the inside fills in on top, and the finished grid reads as one scene. All the text is inside the picture (neon, menu board, ad, sticky note, whiteboard), and every number and line comes from the app itself.

## Daily routine (5 minutes)

1. Post at about 7pm, paste the caption, add the alt text, post, then paste the first comment and pin it.
2. Reply to every comment for the first hour.
3. Share the post to your Story with a sticker (a poll like "Mild, medium or unhinged?" works well on day 2 or 3).
4. Reuse each image on TikTok as a photo post. After post 6, a slow pan across the full street row and then the inside row makes a good 10 second clip (the full panoramas are rendered by the build as `mural-outside.png` and `mural-inside.png`, 3240x1440).

## Check before posting

- If your profile already has other posts, the picture starts at post 1 at the top left, so it still looks intentional, just with older posts below it.
- Post in order. The scene only reads as one painting once all six are up, so do not skip days.
- The bio link: only say "Link in bio" once the link works. Until then, leave "Follow so you do not miss it" as the call to action.
- If you want different dates, nothing on the images depends on them.
