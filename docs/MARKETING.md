# Brokey: marketing and money plan

Written 20 October 2026 for a solo founder with almost no budget. Everything here is cheap, fast and on-brand. Numbers marked *assumption* are guesses to plan with, not forecasts.

## 1. The one-line pitch

> **The budgeting app that roasts you. Every price is in halal snack packs.**

Why it works: it is a joke people can repeat in one breath, it is Melbourne-specific (so locals feel seen), and the core loop makes good short video (a price becomes HSPs, a mascot judges you).

**Who it is for.** First: Melbourne uni students 18 to 24 who overspend on food, nights out and online sales. Second: the same crowd in the rest of Australia. Third: broke Gen Z anywhere (the HSP unit and currency are changeable, so it travels).

**Languages as a wedge.** The app speaks English, Chinese, Indonesian and Spanish. Student associations for those communities are cheap, loyal first audiences, and the Indonesian community in particular is already in your orbit through IFF Australia.

## 3. Brokey Crew: the UGC creator programme

**Who.** 10 to 15 micro-creators (1,000 to 25,000 followers) on TikTok and Instagram Reels: Melbourne uni life, food, thrifting, "broke student" and finance-for-beginners. Micro creators reply, cost little and their audience is exactly local.

**Pay in HSPs.** Cash is tight, so the offer is on-brand: **paid in actual HSPs ($18 each, sent by PayID)**, plus free Plus for life.

| Deal | Pays |
| --- | --- |
| Seed: use the app for a week, post 1 video | 2 HSPs ($36) + Plus for life |
| Standard: 3 videos over 2 weeks | 6 HSPs ($108) |
| Bonus | +1 HSP per 10,000 views on any video, capped at 3 HSPs |

*Budget cap: about A$150 to A$250 for the whole first round.* Nobody gets paid before the app is live. Until then, creators get TestFlight access only.

**Where to find them.** Search TikTok for "unimelb", "monash", "rmit", "melbourne student", "broke student", "halal snack pack melbourne"; check who comments on student-budget videos; ask friends to tag creators they like. DM format: three lines, the mascot picture, "we pay in HSPs".

**Rules for creators (put these in the DM).**
1. Disclose it is paid. Use the platform's paid-partnership tool and `#ad`. Australian ad rules require it.
2. Show your own real spending or a clearly staged joke. No made-up financial claims.
3. Do not name real shops or banks in the roasts, and no body-shaming. Keep it about spending.
4. Tag the app and include the link in bio.
5. Send me the raw file so I can reuse it (with your permission) in ads and the site.

### Content brief: five repeatable formats

1. **"My $X is Y HSPs."** Pick up any item, say the price, flip it to HSPs. Hook: *"This jacket is ten halal snack packs. Ten."*
2. **Roast reaction.** Log a real buy on screen, read the roast out loud, react. Hook: *"I let an app roast my Uber Eats."*
3. **The fridge challenge.** Want something, put it in the cooling-off fridge, come back in 24 hours. Show what you still wanted. Hook: *"I made myself wait 24 hours for the thing I definitely needed."*
4. **Shame shelf reveal.** Show the dumbest thing you bought this year and its price in HSPs. Hook: *"Meet my shame shelf."*
5. **Week recap.** Screen record the week, the sauce bottles squirt, Brokey reacts. Hook: *"My budget app is more disappointed than my mum."*

### Three ready scripts (15 to 20 seconds)

**A. HSP conversion.** Text on screen: *"What things cost in HSPs."* Hold up a bubble tea ($7) "0.4 HSP". Concert ticket $140 "7.8 HSPs". Phone case $45 "2.5 HSPs". Beat. *"Okay. Fine. I will not buy the phone case."* End card: Brokey logo, "Brokey. Free on iPhone."

**B. The roast.** Open on the + button. Type 650, pick "Coffee". Brokey's line pops up. Creator reads it out, stares at the camera. Text: *"It has never been wrong."*

**C. The fridge.** Creator picks up their phone at a checkout: *"I want this."* Tap "I want this". Text: *"24 hours."* Cut to next day: *"Do I still want it?"* Either answer is a win. End card.

**Hooks bank (use as first-line text):** "My budget app roasts me and it is rude." / "How many halal snack packs is your rent?" / "Stop putting it on Afterpay." / "I tried to buy a fourth pair of white sneakers." / "POV: your wallet got a mascot." / "Day 3 of not buying things at 2 am."

## 4. Campus and community

- **Posters.** Print the A3 (see section 6) and ask the student union or club office what the noticeboard rules are before putting anything up. Where postering is not allowed, use the free community boards in cafes and share houses.
- **Stickers.** The mascot is drawn as a sticker. Print a few hundred die-cut stickers with a QR to the App Store page (after approval). Stick them on laptops, hand them out at any club event.
- **Student clubs.** Message the Indonesian, Chinese and Latin American student associations and finance / entrepreneurship clubs. Offer a 5 minute "roast the committee's spending" demo at a meeting. These people share things in group chats, which is where this app spreads.
- **Halal snack pack shops near campus.** Ask one friendly shop for a counter card: *"Spent more than a large HSP on coffee this week? Brokey knows."* Do it with permission only, and do not use a shop's name or logo without their say-so.
- **O-Week stall (Feb 2027).** One table, one banner, a "spin for a roast" wheel, stickers, QR to download. Costs a stall fee set by the club or union; ask early.

## 5. Growth loops already in the app

2. **Roast screenshots.** The roast bubble is designed to be screenshotted. Next release: a share button that adds the Brokey wordmark.
3. **Stickers and streaks.** Reward return visits and give people something to brag about.

## 6. The money plan

### Principle
The core loop stays free and complete: logging, safe-to-spend, roasts, cooling-off, shame shelf. A budgeting app that charges before it helps will lose to a free spreadsheet. Money comes from things people want *more* of, sold once, in student prices.

### Brokey Plus (add in version 1.1, after the first approval)
One **in-app purchase** (Apple requires in-app purchase for unlocking app features, guideline 3.1.1).

| Free | Plus |
| --- | --- |
| Everything in the core loop, 1 wallet, Garlic and BBQ themes, HSP receipt | Unlimited wallets, spreadsheet and PDF exports, the 3AM Kebab Shop neon theme, widgets, shared budgets with friends (v2) |

**Price:** **A$9.99 once** ("half an HSP, forever") with an optional A$2.99 monthly for people who dislike big payments. Cheaper than one HSP is the joke and the anchor.

### Other income, in order of effort
1. **Sticker packs and extra themes** as small one-off purchases (A$1.99 to A$3.99).
2. **Merch:** receipt-style tees and stickers via print-on-demand. Very low effort, mostly for fans and creators.
3. **Sponsored content, not in-app ads.** A student-friendly brand pays a creator to make a Brokey-style video. Never put ads inside the app (the "no ads, no trackers" promise is part of the product and the App Store privacy label).
4. **Universities and student unions.** Money-wellbeing programmes might license a branded version or buy a workshop. Test it with one conversation once you have a few thousand users.
5. **Grants and programmes.** Uni entrepreneurship programmes sometimes give small grants or mentoring to student apps. Worth one application after launch.

### Illustrative maths (*assumptions, not forecasts*)
Apple takes 15% under the Small Business Program (for proceeds up to US$1 million a year). RevenueCat, if you use it to manage purchases, is free until US$2,500 a month of tracked revenue.

| Installs | Buy Plus (assumed 4%) | Gross at A$9.99 | After Apple's 15% |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 500 | 20 | A$200 | about A$170 |
| 2,000 | 80 | A$800 | about A$680 |
| 10,000 | 400 | A$4,000 | about A$3,400 |

Even the good case is pocket money plus a strong portfolio piece. Treat the first goal as **covering the A$150-ish of running costs** and the second as **being able to say "10,000 students use my app"**. Talk to an accountant about your ABN and tax; this is not financial advice.

## 7. What to measure (weekly, 10 minutes)

| Number | What good looks like early |
| --- | --- |
| Installs from each channel (link tags per creator and poster QR) | Which channel is worth repeating |
| Day-1 and day-7 return | Day-7 above 15% is encouraging for a utility app *(assumption)* |
| Buys logged per active user per week | 5 or more means the habit stuck |
| Cooling-off timers that end in "ditched it" | The proof the app changes behaviour. This is the number for your pitch |
| Receipt shares | The loop is working |

The app has no analytics by design. Use App Store Connect (installs, retention, sources), one short in-app survey link later, and per-creator links. If you want deeper numbers, add privacy-first analytics and update the privacy label and manifest first.
